# Ludo 3D Dice Roller - Pro Mobile Edition (with Real Pixabay Audio)

A realistic mobile-first 3D dice rolling application designed for physical Ludo and board games.

## What's New:
1. **Real-World Audio Slicing & Incorporation**:
   - The continuous long audio files were analyzed and precision-sliced into modular audio components:
     - **Seamless Shaking Loop**: Extracted from the most energetic cup-rattling section of `#25620` and crossfaded so it loops seamlessly and naturally as long as you hold down the screen.
     - **Quick Tap Shake**: A compact shake burst from `#90280` that plays on a quick tap/click without holding, so every press has responsive dice-shake audio.
     - **5 Unique Real Rolls**: Extracted from the multiple takes in `#201898`, with randomized selection and pitch modulation each time you roll!
     - **Individual Impact Clacks**: Extracted from initial transients for inter-dice collisions and wooden tray wall bounces.
2. **Dynamic 3D Physics Simulation**:
   - Randomized launch trajectories, inter-dice collisions with momentum transfer, wall deflections, and natural resting scatter.
3. **Responsive & Forgiving Secret Cheat**:
   - Indicator dot on top right shows the target direction:
     - **Red Dot**: 45° angle (Top-Right corner)
     - **Green Dot**: 135° angle (Top-Left corner)
     - **Blue Dot**: 225° angle (Bottom-Left corner)
     - **Yellow Dot**: 315° angle (Bottom-Right corner)
   - Hold anywhere on screen for ~0.3s+ (hearing the real cup shake) and make a subtle move (10px+) in that corner's direction.
   - Haptic vibration buzz and a micro-pulse on the dot confirm the cheat is locked in.
   - Releasing guarantees all sixes (`[6, 6]` on 2 dice, `[6]` on 1 die) and grants you the legitimate Ludo double-six reroll!
